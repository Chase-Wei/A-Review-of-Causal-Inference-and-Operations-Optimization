# Research Plan

## 1. Motivation

Modern operations optimization increasingly relies on observational data. Predictive accuracy alone may be insufficient when the decision changes the data-generating process or when confounding affects the estimated relationship between actions and outcomes.

## 2. Core Themes

### Causal Estimation for Decisions

- Average treatment effects and conditional average treatment effects
- Off-policy evaluation
- Uplift modeling and heterogeneous treatment effects
- Doubly robust and orthogonal estimation

### Optimization With Causal Inputs

- Prescriptive optimization using estimated treatment effects
- Policy learning under constraints
- Resource allocation with causal responses
- Pricing, assortment, and intervention design

### Robustness and Generalization

- Confounding and hidden bias
- Covariate shift and policy-induced distribution shift
- Causal transportability
- Connections with distributionally robust optimization

## 3. Application Areas

- Inventory and supply chain interventions
- Personalized pricing and promotions
- Healthcare operations and treatment allocation
- Platform operations and recommendation policies
- Labor, logistics, and service-system interventions

## 4. Draft Deliverables

- Annotated bibliography
- Concept map of causal inference and operations optimization
- Short review manuscript outline
- Example-driven section on data-driven optimization failures under confounding
